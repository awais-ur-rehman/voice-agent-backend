## Review Focus

- A worker finishes an old generation after re-index/delete or lease takeover: activation must reject it and preserve the correct serving generation (Tasks2–3).
- A cross-tenant corpus dominates the global vector index or a forged test scope names another agent: no foreign content or false authorized-recall starvation (Tasks4–5).
- A question mixes DOB/name/phone with FAQ while verification is pending: no hosted factor leakage, accidental identity change or attempt increment; a later yes after FAQ cannot reuse old confirmation (Task5).
- A source is deleted/re-indexed while embedding/NIM is running: no stale prepared answer commits; locks remain short and ordered (Tasks4–5).
- Browser navigation, retries and background refresh occur during raw upload/replacement/re-index/delete: retain the exact operation without duplicating sources/jobs or losing the parent dirty draft (Task6).
