# Pearl Dental Studio — Demo

Fictional clinic information for a software demonstration; not a real practice.

## What dental services are available?

Pearl Dental Studio Demo offers dental consultations, scaling and polishing, filling assessments, root canal assessments, children’s dental consultations and orthodontic consultations. Dr Ayesha Khan handles general dentistry and children’s consultations. Dr Omar Raza handles orthodontic consultations. Filling, root canal, braces and aligner treatment plans and final treatment quotations require a dentist’s assessment; an assessment appointment is not a promise of treatment that day. Implants, sedation, oral surgery and home visits are not offered in this demo.

## Is parking available?

Free visitor parking is available behind Demo Health Plaza. Use the blue visitor-parking entrance, then follow the Pearl Dental Studio signs to Suite 2. Bicycle racks are beside the visitor entrance. Parking spaces are first come, first served and cannot be reserved by the assistant. This is a fictional demo facility.

## Is the clinic wheelchair accessible?

The fictional demo clinic has a step-free entrance, a wheelchair ramp, a lift to Suite 2, wide reception access and an accessible toilet. A companion may accompany a patient. Tell reception in advance if assistance or additional appointment time is needed. The assistant can describe these facilities but cannot guarantee a specific staff member will be available.

## Which payment methods do you accept?

Pearl Dental Studio Demo accepts cash in Pakistani rupees, debit or credit cards, and bank transfer at reception. An itemized receipt is available after payment. Payment details and card numbers must never be entered into the assistant chat. The demo does not collect online payments, send payment links or provide bank-account details through the assistant. Treatment estimates are confirmed by reception after the dentist’s assessment.

## Do you accept dental insurance?

The demo clinic does not offer direct insurance billing or cashless insurer approval. Patients pay the clinic and may request an itemized receipt for a reimbursement claim with their insurer. Coverage, claim acceptance and reimbursement amounts are determined by the insurer, not the clinic or assistant. Bring any claim-form requirements to reception; the assistant cannot approve a claim.

## Can I bring my child for a dental visit?

Children’s dental consultations are available with Dr Ayesha Khan in this fictional demo. A parent or legal guardian must attend with anyone under 18 and handle the registration and consent discussion with the clinic. Please tell reception the child’s age and any accessibility needs. The assistant does not recommend a treatment, decide whether sedation is needed or provide clinical consent.

## Can you accommodate nervous patients?

The demo clinic offers a quieter waiting area and can note a request for a slower, explanation-focused consultation. A companion is welcome. Tell reception about the request before the visit; availability of additional appointment time must be checked by staff. The assistant cannot promise pain-free treatment, sedation, a particular medicine or a clinical outcome.

## What happens during the first consultation?

At a first consultation, reception checks the registration and the dentist discusses the reason for the visit, reviews relevant information and explains proposed next steps. Any examination, imaging or treatment requires the clinic’s normal consent process. The listed appointment duration is reserved consultation time, not a guaranteed treatment duration. The assistant books only the configured service and does not diagnose or promise same-day treatment.

## Do you offer braces and clear aligners?

Orthodontic consultations with Dr Omar Raza are available in the demo. The consultation is for discussing braces or clear-aligner options after assessment. A full course price, suitability, number of visits and treatment duration are not pre-set in this knowledge base. The dentist must confirm them individually. The assistant may book an orthodontic consultation but cannot quote a full braces package or guarantee eligibility.

## Do you accept walk-in patients?

Appointments are preferred. Walk-in requests are considered only if the reception team finds an available suitable slot; arriving at the clinic does not reserve one. Please arrive ten minutes before a confirmed appointment. If you are running late, tell reception so they can check the schedule; the original slot cannot be guaranteed. There is no 24-hour service or guaranteed same-day appointment in this demo.

## How can I request my dental records or a receipt?

Ask the reception team to arrange a records or receipt request. Staff verify identity and authorization before releasing information through the clinic’s agreed secure channel. The assistant cannot retrieve X-rays, send patient records, reveal another patient’s information or email a document. Do not paste patient documents, full card details or identifying medical history into a public knowledge source.

## Which languages and waiting-room facilities are available?

Reception and the demo assistant support English and Urdu. The waiting area has drinking water, seating, a quieter corner and a family seating area. Complimentary Wi-Fi details can be requested from reception on arrival; no Wi-Fi password is stored in the knowledge base. There is no childcare service. Tell reception in advance if you need communication assistance; an interpreter is not guaranteed.
